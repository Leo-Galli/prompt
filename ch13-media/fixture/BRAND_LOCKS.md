# Brand locks -- Counting TODOs (weekend CLI)

Use these locks when drafting image briefs, slide decks, or short video briefs for the demo product `todocount`. Do not invent other brand colors, marks, or product screenshots.

## Product

- Name: todocount (lowercase, one word)
- One-line job: count TODO and FIXME markers in a tree
- Example command (ASCII only): `todocount --ext .py ./src`

## Visual locks

- Background: flat `#121110` (no gradients, glows, textures, or photos as fill)
- Accent: muted teal `#3FA7A0` for one thin rule or one small mark only
- Type feel: clean sans for titles; monospace only for the example command
- Mark: nested angle brackets with a centered I-beam cursor (describe; do not invent a PNG path)

## Hard negatives

- No fake product UI screenshots
- No fake phone or laptop mockups that invent chrome or menus
- No neon purple, glow trails, or "AI brain" stock tropes
- No emoji
- No stock photo of a smiling developer at a glowing keyboard
- No invented metrics, awards, user counts, or version badges

## Allowed real assets

- Describe the mark and the example command as text in the scene
- If a real screenshot is required later, the human must supply a path; until then write ASSET_REQUIRED

## Accessibility

- Every final image needs alt text that names subject and purpose
- Slide decks need one job per slide and readable title/body contrast intent
